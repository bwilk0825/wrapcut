WrapCut - your site is ready to go online
==========================================

WHAT IS IN THIS ZIP
  index.html             the entire editor (one file, nothing to install)
  wrapcut-library.json   the list of your media
  media/ ...             your files, already renamed correctly

PUT IT ONLINE WITH GITHUB PAGES (free, about 5 minutes)

1. Go to github.com and sign in (a free account is fine).
2. Click the + at the top right and choose "New repository".
3. Name it (for example: wrapcut), choose Public, click "Create repository".
4. On the page that appears, click the link "uploading an existing file".
5. Open the folder you just extracted from this ZIP.
6. Select EVERYTHING inside it (index.html, wrapcut-library.json and the
   whole media folder together) and drag it all onto the GitHub upload page.
7. Click "Commit changes" and wait for the upload to finish.
8. Open Settings > Pages (in the left sidebar). Under "Branch" choose
   "main" and "/ (root)", then click Save.
9. Wait about a minute, then open the address GitHub shows you:
   https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO-NAME/

Done. Everyone who opens that link gets your media in the Media and
Music tabs. Only you can change the library, because only you can
commit to the repository.

TO ADD OR REPLACE FILES LATER
  In your repo click "Add file" > "Upload files", upload the new files
  (same names, or update the JSON too), commit - done.

NOTES
  - The shared library only works on the https:// address above.
    Browsers block file access when opening index.html from disk.
  - Only publish files you have the rights to share.
